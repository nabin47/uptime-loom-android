Everything you need, nothing you don't

Built for people whose workday is tied to a place: offices, campuses, labs, sites.

Starts and stops by itself

Arrive and it starts counting. Leave and it stops. A quick trip out for coffee doesn't split your day.

Multiple work areas

Pick each one on a map, search an address, or share a place from Google Maps. Time counts while you're in any of them.

Plain, useful statistics

Hours vs. your weekly target, what's still needed per day, days present and missed, on-time arrivals, charts and a calendar.

Easy on your battery

Android itself watches your areas and only wakes the app when you arrive or leave. No GPS running all day, no permanent notification.

Private by design

No account, no servers, no ads, no tracking. Your locations and hours never leave your phone.

Fix, mark, export

Edit or add sessions, mark days off for leave or holidays, check in manually, and export everything to CSV. Plus a home-screen widget.

How it works

Three minutes of setup, then you can forget about it.

Install and allow location

Choose "Allow all the time" so arrivals are noticed while the app is closed.

Add your work areas

Search, use your location, or move the map so the pin is on your workplace. Or share a place from Google Maps to Uptime Loom.

Set your week

Weekly target, weekend days and work hours. Then just go to work.

Questions

Will it drain my battery?

No. It uses Android's geofencing, the same low-power system Google recommends for "arrive/leave" features. The phone only wakes the app when you cross into or out of a work area.

Why does it need location "all the time"?

Arriving and leaving happen while the app is closed. Without "Allow all the time" Android won't tell the app you've arrived. Your location is used only on your phone and never uploaded. Prefer not to? Choose "When I open the app" in Settings: open Uptime Loom when you arrive and after you leave, and it counts the time in between.

How accurate are the times?

Usually within a few minutes. Android reports arrivals and departures with a short delay, and the app records the time you actually crossed the boundary. You can edit any session.

My phone has no Google Play services (e.g. some Huawei phones).

It still works: the app checks your location every 15 minutes using the low-power network location, so times are accurate to about 15 minutes.

It doesn't notice when I arrive on my phone.

Some brands stop apps in the background. In the app, open Today → Finish setting up → "Don't restrict in the background", and see dontkillmyapp.com for your phone.

Does it use the internet?

Only while you set up a work area: to show the map (OpenStreetMap data from OpenFreeMap), search addresses, and read shared Google Maps links. Tracking itself works fully offline and nothing about you is ever uploaded.

Is there a desktop version?

Yes, for Windows: Uptime Loom for Windows tracks time from when your PC is on.
