Privacy policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026

Uptime Loom ("the app") is an open-source work-hours tracker. It is designed so that your data stays on your phone.

What the app uses

  • Location, including in the background. The app uses your location to notice when you arrive at and leave the work areas you set, including while the app is closed. This is how it starts and stops counting your work time automatically.
  • Work areas, sessions, days off and settings that you create or that the app records.

What happens to it

  • Everything is stored only on your device. The app has no servers and no account system, and it does not upload, sell or share any data.
  • The app contains no advertising and no analytics or tracking libraries.
  • Location fixes are processed on the device by Android (and, on phones that have them, Google Play services) to detect entering or leaving a work area. Only the resulting times are stored, not a history of your location.
  • Setting up a work area is the only time the app uses the internet:
    • The map is loaded from OpenFreeMap (OpenStreetMap data). Like any map, the tile server sees which map area is being viewed and your IP address.
    • Searching for an address uses Android's built-in geocoder, which may send the search text to your phone's geocoding provider (for example Google).
    • If you paste or share a short Google Maps link (maps.app.goo.gl), the app asks Google where that link points so it can read the coordinates.
    The app itself has no server and receives none of this. Tracking your arrival and departure never uses the internet.
  • Android's own device backup or phone-to-phone transfer may include the app's data so it survives a phone change. This is controlled by your phone's settings and your backup account; the developer has no access to it.
  • When you choose "Export", the app writes a CSV file to the location you pick. What happens to that file afterwards is up to you.

Your control

  • You can turn off location access at any time in Android settings; automatic tracking then stops.
  • Delete work areas, sessions and days off in the app, or remove everything by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling it.

Children

The app is not directed at children.

Changes
